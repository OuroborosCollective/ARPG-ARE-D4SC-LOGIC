# Delivery scope

## Included

The owner requests the complete playable journey from the current Aschenzoll endpoint through a distant Immergrün and two further areas, with connecting combat areas, quests, NPCs, scenery, equipment loot and optional 3–6-floor regenerating caves in every connecting area. Finish the current Fadenwerk acceptance before mutating the game for this expansion. Deliver in revision-bound vertical increments; the overall scope is not complete when only the first increment exists.

The first narrative anchor is the apparent freight inspector in the next trading post/village outside the kingdom. He is a foreign spy and trades investigative help for Immergrün troop strength and march plans. Keep acquisition, knowledge, possession and deliberate personal disclosure separate. The information might enable an attack; neither an actual attack nor its targets/outcome has been approved.

Retain the mill/Rattler and bear-family decisions, both Aschenzoll evidence branches, optional hunter status and existing quest/save history. Backfill side-cave access for retained connecting areas where necessary, without turning the fixed bear-family nest into a resetting combat room. Use the available licensed assets, Fable HUD and World Forge/Fadenwerk authoring boundaries. Final balancing and polish follow playable coverage, but correctness, reachable layouts, safe spawn, loot/save integrity and basic tablet readability are required throughout.

## Excluded

Automatic game/store publication, new purchases, multiplayer, a new gameplay authority, rewriting the current menus/camera, resetting completed story, invented king guilt, fabricated runtime/device evidence. Names of unapproved kingdoms, the spy's promised disclosure, Immergrün's main encounter and the story purpose of the two following areas remain owner decisions, not silently invented canon.

## Risks and dependencies

- The current world uses EntryAct 1–10 and a dedicated three-floor crypt. A general journey and side-cave adapter must preserve existing AC1–AC19/load semantics and avoid converting old story runs silently.
- Current cave chest fragments are not a complete guarantee of increasing loot value. Specify and prove depth scaling and catalog receipts, including BigInteger progression and causal companion XP.
- Native Unity is on the VPS, not in this local source-only checkout. A Core run is not a Unity or Android build.
- Overall requirements retain unresolved lore questions; independent technical planning may proceed, dependent story implementation waits for the owner.
